Lasagna Recipe Yummy Yummers

Description

Lasagna is a delicious dish originated from ancient Greece and Rome, though in modern day it is part of the diet of many people from around the globe.

Ingredients:

  • Half a pound of ground pork
  • Half a pound of lean ground beef
  • A diced onion
  • A can of crushed tomatoes
  • A can of tomato sauce
  • 2 chopped tablespoons of parsley
  • 1 crushed clove of garlic
  • A pinch of sugar
  • Dried basil
  • Dried oregano
  • Salt
  • Ground black pepper
  • Uncooked lasagna noodles
  • Cottage cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Three eggs
