Lasagna Recipe Yummy Yummers

Description

Lasagna is a delicious dish originated from ancient Greece and Rome, though in modern day it is part of the diet of many people from around the globe.

Ingredients:

  • Half a pound of ground pork
  • Half a pound off lean ground beef
  • Half a cup of minced onion
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can of tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons of chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 and a half tablespoons of dried basil
  • 1 and a half tablespoons of salt
  • Halff a tablespoon of dried oregano
  • 1 eight of a tablespoon of white sugar
  • 1 package of lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound small-curd cottage cheese
  • 3 fourths of a cup of grated parmesan cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons of salt
  • 1 fourth of a teaspoon of ground black pepper
  • 1 package of shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps:

  1. Combine pork and ground beef in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat; cook and stir until browned and crumbly.
  2. Add onion and cook until translucent.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, garlic, basil, salt, oregano, and sugar. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 30 minutes.
  4. While the sauce is simmering, bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in the boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain and set aside. While the noodles are cooking, preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  5. Mix cottage cheese, Parmesan cheese, eggs, remaining 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, salt, and pepper in a large bowl until combined.
  6. Assemble lasagna: Spread a spoon or two of sauce over the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish just to to coat it. Place two layers of noodles over the sauce to cover.
  7. Layer with 1/2 of the cheese mixture, 1/2 of the remaining sauce, and 1/2 of the mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers once more using the remaining noodles, cheese mixture, sauce, and mozzarella. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil.
  8. Bake in the preheated oven for 30 to 40 minutes. Remove the foil and bake until cheese is golden brown, 5 to 10 more minutes.
  9. Remove from the oven and let stand for 10 minutes before cutting and serving
