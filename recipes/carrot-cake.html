Brazilian Carrot Cake Recipe

Description

The Brazilian carrot cake is an immensely popular dish in Brazil, of course. Despite the seemingly odd combination of Carrots and Chocolate topping, the dish never fails to steal the hearts of many who've tried it before.

Ingredients

    Cake

  • 300g of carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 250g granulated sugar
  • 250ml of vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  • 250g of plain flour
  • 1 tablespoon of baking powder
  • 1/4 of a tablespoon of salt

    • Chocolate topping

  • 1 can of condensed milk (397g)
  • 150g of dark chocolate
  • 150ml of double cream
  • 50g of unsalted butter
  • 1/8th of a tablespoon of salt
